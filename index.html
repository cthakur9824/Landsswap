Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying, selling, and swapping land on Landsswap.

Land swapping is the exchange of one property for another between two owners, potentially with an equalization payment if the values differ. On Landsswap, you list your property, find a property you're interested in, and propose a swap. If both parties agree on value, our platform facilitates the legal documentation, title transfer, and closing process. Equalization payments handle any value gap between the two properties.

Creating an account and browsing listings is completely free. Listing a property is free for your first listing. Premium listings with enhanced visibility and professional photography are available for a small fee. We charge a competitive transaction fee only when a sale or swap closes successfully — no upfront costs, no hidden charges.

Every property listed on Landsswap undergoes a preliminary title search through our network of licensed title companies. We verify ownership records, check for liens and encumbrances, confirm legal descriptions, and validate zoning designations. For transactions, a full title insurance policy is issued to protect the buyer. Our verification badge on a listing means the title has been cleared through this process.

Absolutely! Interstate land swaps are common on our platform. Each state has its own recording and transfer requirements, but our legal team and network of attorneys handles the compliance for both states. We coordinate cross-state title searches, deed preparation, and recording so both transfers close simultaneously through our escrow service.

You can list virtually any type of real property: residential lots, commercial parcels, agricultural and farm land, industrial sites, recreational properties, timberland, ranchland, vacant raw land, mixed-use parcels, and even fractional ownership interests. Each category has tailored fields for relevant details like soil types, water rights, mineral rights, easements, and zoning classifications.

Once both parties agree to terms, funds (or equalization payments) are deposited into a licensed escrow account managed by an independent escrow officer. The escrow holds all funds until: (1) title clearance is confirmed, (2) all contingencies are met (surveys, inspections, etc.), and (3) deeds are prepared and signed. Only then are funds disbursed and deeds recorded. This protects both buyer and seller throughout the transaction.

Yes! Land swaps can qualify as 1031 like-kind exchanges under IRS rules, allowing you to defer capital gains tax. Our platform works with qualified intermediaries to structure transactions as 1031 exchanges when appropriate. We handle the strict timelines (45-day identification, 180-day closing) and documentation requirements. Always consult with a tax professional for your specific situation.
